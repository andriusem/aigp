# AIGP exam study tips

Three-page IAPP guide for exam takers. Original: [PDF](../../_library/originals/AIGP%20EXAM%20STUDY%20TIPS.pdf).

**Exam format** (as stated in the guide):
- 100 multiple-choice questions, 85 scored and 15 unscored trial items. Mostly single answer; some multi-select; some scenario-based.
- 2.75 hours, about 1.65 minutes per question.
- Scaled score 100–500; 300 passes.

**Strategy, in short:** use the BoK as the checklist and weight study by question counts; master Domain I early; for scenarios, place the situation in the AI life cycle and pick the action that fits that phase; watch qualifiers (NOT, EXCEPT, LEAST, MOST, "first"); practise under time pressure.

**Other resources it recommends:** the IAPP Key Terms for AI Governance glossary (aligned with Domain I), IAPP news and trackers, and the candidate handbook.
