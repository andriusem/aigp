# NIST

**In one sentence:** the US National Institute of Standards and Technology, a federal agency in the Department of Commerce, which publishes the voluntary AI Risk Management Framework, the most widely used AI risk framework outside the EU.

## What it publishes
- **AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0, NIST AI 100-1, January 2023):** voluntary and sector-neutral. Part 1 frames AI risk, the actors in the AI value chain and the characteristics of trustworthy AI; Part 2 (the core) has four functions: **Govern, Map, Measure, Manage**. [Book §8.3.1](../../_library/text/ai-governance-strategy-to-execution/ch08-ai-industry-standards-and-guidance.md#8.3.1%20AI%20Risk%20Management%20Framework)
- **AI RMF Playbook:** suggested actions per function. [Book §8.3.2](../../_library/text/ai-governance-strategy-to-execution/ch08-ai-industry-standards-and-guidance.md#8.3.2%20AI%20RMF%20Playbook)
- **Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1, July 2024):** applies the framework to genAI risks. [Book §8.3.3](../../_library/text/ai-governance-strategy-to-execution/ch08-ai-industry-standards-and-guidance.md#8.3.3%20GenAI%20Profile)
- **Status (as of 2026-11-19):** NIST states AI RMF 1.0 is being updated and a revised version is in progress, following the July 2025 US AI Action Plan. [NIST AIRC](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/) Check for a new version before relying on details.

## In practice
- The default structure for an AI risk management programme when there is no binding law (most US clients), and a useful operating model alongside the EU AI Act's risk-management requirement.
- Govern sets policies, roles and culture across the whole programme; Map establishes context and identifies risks; Measure assesses them; Manage prioritises and treats them.
- Voluntary: following it does not by itself prove legal compliance, but it is widely recognised as good practice.

## Connected
- EU AI Act [Art. 9 risk management system](../../_library/text/eu-ai-act/art-009-risk-management-system.md): the binding EU counterpart for high-risk systems; the NIST functions can be used to organize it.
- [OECD](oecd.md): international principles that NIST's trustworthiness characteristics echo.
