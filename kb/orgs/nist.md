# NIST

**In one sentence:** the US National Institute of Standards and Technology, a federal agency in the Department of Commerce that writes voluntary technical standards and frameworks, including the AI Risk Management Framework.

## What it contributes to AI governance
- **AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0, NIST AI 100-1, January 2023):** voluntary, sector-neutral. Part 1 frames AI risk, the actors in the AI value chain and the characteristics of trustworthy AI; Part 2, the core, has four functions: **Govern, Map, Measure, Manage**. [Book §8.3.1](../../_library/text/ai-governance-strategy-to-execution/ch08-ai-industry-standards-and-guidance.md#8.3.1%20AI%20Risk%20Management%20Framework)
- **AI RMF Playbook:** suggested actions for each part of the core. [Book §8.3.2](../../_library/text/ai-governance-strategy-to-execution/ch08-ai-industry-standards-and-guidance.md#8.3.2%20AI%20RMF%20Playbook)
- **Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1, July 2024):** applies the AI RMF to genAI risks. [Book §8.3.3](../../_library/text/ai-governance-strategy-to-execution/ch08-ai-industry-standards-and-guidance.md#8.3.3%20GenAI%20Profile)
- **Status (Nov 2026):** NIST says AI RMF 1.0 is being updated and a revised version is in progress, following the July 2025 US AI Action Plan. Learn 1.0 for the exam.

## Exam angle
BoK II.D asks you to understand the NIST AI RMF and related tools. Know the four functions and that Govern runs across the other three. Voluntary, not law.
