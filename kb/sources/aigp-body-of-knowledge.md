# AIGP Body of Knowledge v2.1

Defines the four exam domains, their competencies and performance indicators, and the minimum and maximum number of questions per domain. Questions mostly test the remember/understand and apply/analyze levels of Bloom's taxonomy. Original: [PDF](../../_library/originals/AIGP_Cert_BOK_13March2026_FINAL.pdf).
