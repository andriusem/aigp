# OECD Framework for the Classification of AI Systems

**In one sentence:** an [OECD](../orgs/oecd.md) tool for describing any AI system along five dimensions, so that organizations and policymakers can compare systems, build inventories and assess risks consistently.

## The five dimensions
1. **People and planet:** who uses or is affected by the system; impact on human rights, wellbeing and the environment.
2. **Economic context:** the sector and business function, how critical the deployment is, its scale and maturity.
3. **Data and input:** what data the system uses, its origin, how it is collected, its quality.
4. **AI model:** the technical model: how it is built and trained, how it reasons.
5. **Task and output:** what the system does (e.g. recognition, forecasting), what it produces, the actions that follow, and how autonomously.

## In practice
- **AI inventory or register:** use the five dimensions as the column structure, so every system is described the same way.
- **Risk triage:** the people-and-planet and economic-context dimensions show who could be harmed and how much is at stake; data and model show where technical risks sit; task and output show how much autonomy and human oversight there is.
- **Bridging to law:** the description feeds into legal classification (e.g. whether a use falls in an EU AI Act high-risk area), but does not replace it.
