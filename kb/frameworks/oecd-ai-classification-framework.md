# OECD Framework for the Classification of AI Systems

**In one sentence:** an [OECD](../orgs/oecd.md) tool for describing any AI system along five dimensions, so policymakers and organizations can compare systems and assess their risks.

## The five dimensions
1. **People and planet:** who uses or is affected by the system, and its impact on rights, wellbeing and the environment.
2. **Economic context:** the sector and business function it is deployed in, how critical it is, its scale and maturity.
3. **Data and input:** what data the system uses, where it comes from, how it is collected and its quality.
4. **AI model:** the technical model: how it was built and trained and how it reasons.
5. **Task and output:** what the system does (e.g. recognition, forecasting), what it produces, the actions that follow and how autonomous those actions are.

## What it is for
A common understanding of AI, a baseline for AI registries and inventories, a starting point for sector-specific frameworks, and support for risk assessment and management.

## Exam angle
"Select all that apply" on the five dimensions. A distractor might be a plausible-sounding dimension that is not on the list (e.g. "legal jurisdiction").
