# EU AI Act

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, as published in the Official Journal on 12 July 2024. Original: [PDF](../../_library/originals/EU-ai-act.pdf). Reading copy: [index of all articles](../../_library/text/eu-ai-act/00-index.md), one file per article (113), annex (13) and the [recitals](../../_library/text/eu-ai-act/recitals.md) (180). Cross-references between articles are links.

Later amendments (such as the Digital Omnibus) are not in this text; note them in the knowledge notes where they change something. Only the Official Journal version is authentic.
