# AIGP training BoK mapping v2.1.0

Annotated version of the [Body of Knowledge](aigp-body-of-knowledge.md) from the training course: each performance indicator is mapped to the course module and lesson that covers it (e.g. M3/L2), with space for notes, examples and a hi/med/low confidence rating. The module mapping is in the **Course** column of [progress.md](../../study/progress.md). Original: [PDF](../../_library/originals/AIGP_BoK_map_v2.4.3.pdf). Not for reproduction.

One difference from the BoK: the mapping lists II.C at 5–7 questions, the BoK v2.1 at 6–8. We follow the BoK.
