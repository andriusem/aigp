# OECD

**In one sentence:** the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development, an intergovernmental body of mainly high-income democracies based in Paris, which set the first intergovernmental standard for trustworthy AI.

## What it contributes to AI governance
- **OECD AI Principles** (2019, updated 2024): values every AI system should uphold, adopted by close to 50 countries. Formally a Council Recommendation (legal instrument OECD-LEGAL-0449). Backbone of book chapter 1. [Book §1.3](../../_library/text/ai-governance-strategy-to-execution/ch01-why-ai-needs-to-be-governed.md#1.3%20OECD%20AI%20Principles)
- **Definition of an AI system:** the OECD's definition (revised in 2023) is the model for the EU AI Act's definition in [Art. 3(1)](../../_library/text/eu-ai-act/art-003-definitions.md).
- **Classification framework:** [five dimensions for describing AI systems](../frameworks/oecd-ai-classification-framework.md).
- **Catalogue of tools and metrics for trustworthy AI:** technical, procedural and educational tools plus measurement methods, organized around the principles. [Book §8.5.1](../../_library/text/ai-governance-strategy-to-execution/ch08-ai-industry-standards-and-guidance.md#8.5.1%20OECD)
- **OECD.AI Policy Observatory:** tracks national AI policies and incidents.

## Exam angle
BoK II.D asks you to understand the OECD principles, framework, policies and recommended practices. OECD guidance is voluntary (soft law), unlike the EU AI Act.
